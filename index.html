About

Glioblastoma is the most common and most aggressive primary brain tumor in adults, and its prognosis has changed little in two decades. Part of the reason is that the tumor is not only a mass of malignant cells: it recruits and reshapes the immune, vascular and neural tissue around it.

This workshop brings together cancer biologists, neuroscientists, engineers and clinicians to look at glioblastoma as a whole system, from the molecular programs of tumor cells and their microenvironment to imaging, surgery and new therapeutic strategies.

The GENTE project

GENTE, a collaboration between the CNR Institute of Neuroscience in Pisa and the Sant'Anna School of Advanced Studies, tests whether visual stimulation in the gamma band (GENUS, Gamma ENtrainment Using Sensory stimulation) can reactivate the parvalbumin-positive interneurons damaged by glioblastoma and slow tumor growth. The goal is a non-invasive neuromodulation protocol with potential clinical application.
